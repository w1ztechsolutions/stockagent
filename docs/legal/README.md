## Key Legal Framework

The following Malawian laws apply to this project:

### Data Protection
- **Data Protection Act, 2024** (Act No. 3 of 2024) — Governs personal data processing
- **Electronic Transactions and Cybersecurity Act No. 33 of 2016** — Cybersecurity and electronic transactions
- **Constitution of Malawi, Section 21** — Right to privacy

### Financial Services
- **Securities Act, 2010** (Act No. 20 of 2010) — Regulates securities markets
- **Financial Services Act, 2010** — Regulates financial institutions
- **Companies Act, 2013** (Act No. 15 of 2013) — Company law

### Consumer Protection
- **Consumer Protection Act** (Chapter 48:10) — Consumer rights and protections

### Regulatory Bodies
- **Data Protection Authority (DPA)** — info@dpa.mw | +265 991 80 21 80
- **Reserve Bank of Malawi (RBM)** — www.rbm.mw
- **Malawi Stock Exchange (MSE)** — www.mse.co.mw

## Important Disclaimers

1. **These documents do not constitute legal advice.** They are provided for transparency and compliance purposes. For specific legal questions, consult a qualified attorney in Malawi.

2. **The project is non-commercial.** Market data from the MSE is provided for personal, non-commercial use only. Commercial use requires a data licensing agreement with the MSE.

3. **The content does not constitute investment advice.** Users should consult qualified financial professionals before making investment decisions.

4. **AI-generated content has limitations.** See the AI Agents Disclaimer for details on risks and limitations of AI assistance.
