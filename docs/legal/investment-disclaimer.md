- Recognize that all investing involves risk, including loss of principal

### 3.2 MSE-Specific Risks

Investments on the Malawi Stock Exchange carry additional risks:

- **Market Risk** — Prices fluctuate based on market conditions
- **Liquidity Risk** — Some counters may have low trading volume, making it difficult to buy or sell
- **Concentration Risk** — The market has only 17 listed companies
- **Currency Risk** — All investments are denominated in Malawi Kwacha (MWK)
- **Regulatory Risk** — Changes in Malawian financial regulations
- **Country Risk** — Economic, political, and currency risks specific to Malawi
