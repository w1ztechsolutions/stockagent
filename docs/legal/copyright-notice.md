> "Unless otherwise stated, the Website and Data is the copyright of the Malawi Stock Exchange."
> — MSE Terms of Use

### 2.2 Non-Commercial Use

Market data is provided for **personal and non-commercial use only**, per the MSE Terms of Use:

> "Access to and use of the Website and the Data is for personal and non-commercial use only. No part of the Website or Data may be copied, reproduced, republished, downloaded, posted, broadcast or transmitted in any way for any commercial purpose and/or exploited for profit."
> — MSE Terms of Use

### 2.3 Required Attribution

When displaying or sharing MSE-sourced market data, the following attribution must be included:

> "Data sourced from the Malawi Stock Exchange (mse.co.mw). Provided 'as is' without warranty of accuracy, timeliness, or completeness."

For derived data or analytics, add:

> "Values computed by Phungu Insights from raw data sourced from the Malawi Stock Exchange."

### 2.4 Commercial Data Licensing

Commercial use of Malawi Stock Exchange data requires a **separate data licensing agreement** with the Malawi Stock Exchange:

> "Those that intend to use the website and data for commercial purposes must first notify the Exchange to sign a data agreement."
> — MSE Terms of Use
